4. Deep Sky

Deep Sky's conversation runs on your device: with Apple Intelligence where your device supports it, and otherwise with Numina's built-in guide. Nothing is downloaded and no conversation leaves your device. Text written by Apple Intelligence is generated automatically and may occasionally be odd; puzzle answers and scoring are always decided by Numina's own engine, not by a model. Deep Sky's puzzles are written in English.
